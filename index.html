ABOUT THE EVENT

Where AI meets modern software engineering.

IEEE NEXUS–2K26 Series II is a national-level one-day symposium focused on the rapidly evolving intersection of Artificial Intelligence, DevOps, cloud-native engineering, automation and responsible technology.

The event provides a platform for knowledge exchange through expert sessions, technical discussions, demonstrations and networking among academia, students and industry.

OBJECTIVES

What participants will explore

01

AI-Powered DevOps

Understand how AI and machine learning can improve development, testing, deployment and operations.

02

Intelligent Automation

Explore intelligent CI/CD pipelines, predictive monitoring and automated software delivery.

03

Cloud & Platform Engineering

Examine cloud-native architectures, containers, orchestration and scalable engineering practices.

04

Responsible Innovation

Discuss security, governance, explainability and responsible use of AI in engineering workflows.
